## 2. Request to the sponsor (via the owner)

The owner is asked to obtain answers to these questions. None asks for confidential content to be shared in this repository.

1. Can a sanitized export of stored submissions be released to the project's controlled environment, with identifier, submission date, title and text? Approximately how many records, from what period, and in which languages (ISS-002, ISS-007, ISS-023)?
2. Which solution-example sources should R2 search: sponsor-held records, external sources, or both? Is any of them available to the project, in what format and under what terms (ISS-003, ISS-037)?
3. Can sponsor assessors judge relevance for the pilot and the test pools, and roughly how many hours are available (ISS-012)?
4. May assessor-approved query text derived from a submission be sent to external patent services (ISS-033)?
5. Are there conditions on storage, retention and deletion of the export in the project environment (ISS-036)?

A candidate failing the licence check is dropped, not used provisionally.

## 5. Separation and handling rules

1. Track S data stays in the controlled environment (AS-04); it is never copied into this repository, into Track P collections or into external queries without authorization (DEC-028 item 10).
2. Track S and Track P results are reported in separate tables. A combined average across tracks is not computed.
3. Every test collection is versioned with its source, licence note, track label and the split dates used (AD-11 applied to evaluation data).
4. Section 8, Section 12 and Section 17 text must name the track behind every statement about results (CLM-045, ISS-028).
