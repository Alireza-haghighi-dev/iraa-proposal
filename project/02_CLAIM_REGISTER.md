- **Purpose:** Tracks every claim that the proposal may rely on, its evidence and its permitted use. Seeded in S01 with claims in the reference documents that must not be used as verified facts.

## Status key

`VERIFIED` (supported by a logged source) · `VERIFIED (vendor statement)` / `VERIFIED IN PART (vendor statement)` (a T2 source confirms the vendor says this; not evidence that the feature works or performs) · `SOURCE CLAIM — UNVERIFIED` (stated in a reference document, not independently checked) · `HYPOTHESIS` (expected effect to be tested) · `PROPOSED` (team proposal) · `OPEN` · `SUPERSEDED` (kept for history; pointer given)

**Evidence depth (added S04.1).** `VERIFIED` means the statement was found in the source at the depth shown in the last column, taken from `02_SOURCE_LOG.md`: **A** = abstract, landing page or single documentation page; **P** = full-text excerpt; **B** = bibliographic record. No claim has yet been checked against the full text of a paper or standard. A-depth claims are abstract-level: figures and wording must pass the S08 Pass A full-text check (ISS-029) before the proposal relies on them. "Partial" marks a source where only part of the statement was found.
